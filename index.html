Card Trick

Five-card protocol · information-theoretic MPC · den Boer 1989

Cut five face-down cards and reveal only the AND of two secret bits.

WHY IT MATTERS

Most cryptography bets that attacks are too expensive. Here every possible secret looks identical — so computing power buys nothing.
